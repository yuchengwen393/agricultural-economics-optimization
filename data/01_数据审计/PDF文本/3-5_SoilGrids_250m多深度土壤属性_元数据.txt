SoilGrids250m 2.0 - Volumetric Water Content at 10kPa aggregated 5000m
Volumetric Water Content at 10kPa suction in 10-3 cm3cm-3 (0.1 v% or 1 mm/m) at 6 standard depths. Predictions were derived using a digital soil mapping approach based on Quantile 
Random Forest, drawing on a global compilation of soil profile data and environmental layers. This map is the result of resampling the mean SoilGrids 250 m predictions (Turek et al. 2021) 
for each 5000 m cell.
